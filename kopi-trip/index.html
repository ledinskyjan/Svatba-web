Where it comes from

Six growing regions you can taste — and why the way coffee is processed here shapes the cup more than the region does.

Giling basah

Indonesia's wet-hulled method: the parchment comes off while the bean still holds 30–35% moisture. You get blue-green beans, low acidity, heavy body and that unmistakable earthy, cedar, spiced cup. If someone tells you Indonesian coffee tastes flat, they drank stale robusta, not this.

Field notes

The overland route

Most people fly Lombok to Labuan Bajo and never see the island in between. Going overland means two ferries — Kayangan to Poto Tano, then Sape to Labuan Bajo — and a full day's driving across Sumbawa. There is coffee the whole way, in Taliwang, Maluk, Sumbawa Besar, Dompu and Bima, and almost none of it appears in any guide.

Time it around the ferries rather than the cafés. The Flores crossing leaves Sape in the morning, so plan to sleep there.

Coffee on the Komodo boat

A liveaboard gives you a 3-in-1 sachet and nothing else. Hot water, however, is never a problem. Bring 250 g ground in Labuan Bajo and a dripper or an AeroPress and you'll have the best cup within fifty nautical miles.

Kampung Komodo and Rinca

Don't go looking for specialty here. What a warung will give you is kopi hitam the local way: coarse grounds, hot water poured straight in, silt on the bottom. Let it settle a minute and stop before the last mouthful. Say "tanpa gula" up front if you don't want it sweet.

The Kintamani ridge

A string of cafés along the crater rim with Batur in front of you. The coffee is uneven and the view isn't — and the beans grow directly below. Worth the drive for the beans and the landscape rather than the brewing.

Before you go

Skip kopi luwak

The tourist luwak farms on Bali keep civets caged and force-feed them. What lands on the tasting tray after the plantation tour tends to be roasted dark enough to hide an unremarkable bean anyway. The same money buys you three excellent single-origin filters.

Buying beans with carry-on only

Beans are heavier than they look — count on the extra weight before you commit to a third bag, especially with cabin baggage only. Make the ones you do take things you can't get elsewhere: Flores Juria, Manggarai Colol, Kintamani.

Look for a roast date, not an expiry date. Humidity kills coffee fast here — take a bag from the back of the shelf and decant it into something airtight when you get home.

Cash on the Gilis, and hours everywhere

Smaller places on all three Gilis are cash only and the ATMs on Trawangan are regularly empty. Draw enough on Lombok before you board.

Check opening hours before you set out — they shift with the season and plenty of places shut around 5 pm. The Map button on each card opens Google Maps.
